# smardo2018amca: Rain and Hurricane Resistant Louvers

**Source:** https://www.amca.org/resources/Smardo%20-%20Louvers%20ASET-US-2018%20-%20RC.pdf
**Evidence boundary:** Conference slides by a louver manufacturer at the standards body's conference. Read in full.

## Sensors

None.

### Pros

- Describes the AMCA 500-L water penetration and wind-driven rain tests.

### Cons

- Commercial product data, no geometry study.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Metal louvers about 1.2 m square.

### Pros

- A 57% free-area still-air louver rejected only 65% of rain at 29 mph and 3 in/h.
- Wind-driven-rain louvers with 43 to 44% free area rejected 99.7 to 99.8%.
- Rain rejection comes from closer blade spacing, lower free area and higher pressure drop.

### Cons

- Building-scale blades; surface tension in millimetre holes is not represented.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports the trade between free area and rain rejection.
