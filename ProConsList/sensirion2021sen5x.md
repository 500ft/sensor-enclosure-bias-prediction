# sensirion2021sen5x: SEN5x Mechanical Design and Assembly Guidelines, Version 1.0

**Source:** https://sensirion.com/resource/user_guide/sen5x/mechanical_design_assembly_guidelines
**Evidence boundary:** Manufacturer guidance across three sensor generations. Read in full. No test data.

## Sensors

Sensirion SPS30, SEN5x, SEN6x.

### Pros

- Gives opening areas for SEN6x: inlet at least 56 mm2, outlet at least 148 mm2.

### Cons

- Guidance changes between versions.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Generic device housings.

### Pros

- Seal inlet from outlet and from the box interior; sensor close to the shell with short ducts.
- Keep away from heat sources and mount below them; SEN6x recommends less than 5 K over-temperature.

### Cons

- SPS30 and SEN5x recommend openings facing down; the 2026 SEN6x note warns that facing up or down accelerates dust build-up.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports sealed sensor inlets and openings at least the sensor's own size.
- Leaves downward orientation open.
