| --- | --- | --- |

## Out

- Generic housekeeping, a second solver, new frameworks and toolchain installation
  as ways to occupy owner waiting time; no demonstrated need in this iteration.
- New CAD, fabricated measurements, historic-data replacement, or declaring a
  model validated from intake success; evidence and scope do not support them.
- New paper searches or a third owner questionnaire; current sources and forms
  already support this bounded plan.
- A software deadline for owner/PI replies or physical weather coverage; elapsed
  time cannot supply those inputs.

## Milestone watch

At core delivery and each owner response, check: (1) A1's generation-mode refusal
regression and full checks; (2) accepted historic provenance in EN-S02;
(3) prospective pilot decision plus explicit ledger dependency disposition;
(4) an approved physical need and actual geometry before CAD promotion.
When a trigger fires, review current evidence and deliberately update the existing
ledger. It is a reason to consider the work, not automatic execution authority.
