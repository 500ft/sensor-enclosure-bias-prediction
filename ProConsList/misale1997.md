# misale1997: A practical formula for air-cooled boards in ventilated enclosures

**Source:** https://www.electronics-cooling.com/1997/09/a-practical-formula-for-air-cooled-boards-in-ventilated-enclosures/
**Evidence boundary:** Trade-magazine summary of the author's experiments; not peer reviewed. Equation images did not render, so the loss correlation was not read.

## Sensors

None. Six heated circuit cards at 5, 10 and 15 W.

### Pros

- Tests vent area, hole shape and open-area ratio together.

### Cons

- Watts of internal heat and up to 50 C rise, far above a sensor box.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Cabinet 100 x 152 x 254 mm; vent areas 600, 800 and 1000 mm2; round holes, horizontal and vertical slots; open-area ratios 0.2 and 0.33; bottom inlet, top outlet.

### Pros

- Equal inlet and outlet area gave the minimum air temperature rise.
- Moving the outlet to the wall opposite the inlet gave the same thermal behaviour.

### Cons

- Indoor still air, no wind, screens or rain protection.
- Optimum is for heat removal, which differs from air exchange for a sensor.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports equal vent areas and a measured chimney height as design inputs.
- Cannot justify a hole count; the worked example (32 holes of 5 mm per vent) is for 10 W.
