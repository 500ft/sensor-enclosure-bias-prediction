# masters2006soffit: Preliminary Investigation of Wind-Driven Rain Intrusion Through Soffits

**Source:** https://www.ihrc.fiu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/HLMP_Year06_Section2_SoffitTesting_RCMPY6v2.pdf
**Evidence boundary:** Full-scale test report. Read in full.

## Sensors

Water collection.

### Pros

- Compares downward-facing vent types, screens, deflectors and a baffle.

### Cons

- Hurricane winds; drop size not controlled.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Soffit vents on a wall and roof mock-up, 72 to 115 mph wind, about 28 to 38 in/h rain.

### Pros

- Downward-facing soffits still admitted 0.3 to 8.6% of incident rain.
- An inner insect screen cut entry by 79 to 86%; deflectors by 69 to 79%.
- Free area did not rank intrusion.

### Cons

- The one baffle tested was the worst (10.9 to 22.1%).
- Wet or dry starting state changed results greatly.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports a second stage behind each opening.
- Does not support assuming a baffle helps without testing.
