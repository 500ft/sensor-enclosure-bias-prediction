# valera2006: Aerodynamic analysis of several insect-proof screens used in greenhouses

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.5424/sjar/2006044-204
**Evidence boundary:** Laboratory experiment. Read in full.

## Sensors

Pressure and velocity instruments in a suction rig and wind tunnel.

### Pros

- Eleven commercial meshes measured from below 1 m/s to about 8 m/s.

### Cons

- Woven greenhouse mesh in a duct.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Meshes with open-area ratio 0.288 to 0.483 and thread diameter 165 to 271 micrometres (Table 1).

### Pros

- Pressure drop fits a*u^2 + b*u + c; densest meshes have about 3 to 4 times the coefficients of the most open ones (Table 2).

### Cons

- No data below about 0.1 m/s, the range of buoyancy-driven flow in a box.
- Fits disagree with an earlier study over a wider porosity range.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports measuring flow with and without the actual mesh on the box.
- Cannot predict mesh loss at buoyancy speeds; the review's estimate is an extrapolation.
