# gore2011cycling: Temperature cycling and pressure in vented and unvented enclosures (PTV-034-R1)

**Source:** https://www.gore.com/sites/default/files/resources/pdf/2025-07/ptv-techinfo-temperature-cycling-us.pdf
**Evidence boundary:** Manufacturer demonstration with no stated uncertainty. Read in full. A Gore PolyVent datasheet (2025) was also read for airflow ratings.

## Sensors

Pressure gauge.

### Pros

- Shows pressure in vented and unvented boxes during heating and cooling.

### Cons

- Marketing demo.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Two 4000 cm3 enclosures, 20 to 50 C and 20 to -5 C in 90 s.

### Pros

- The unvented box swung about plus or minus 1.5 psi; the vented one stayed near zero.
- Cooling vacuum can draw liquid in.

### Cons

- No replication or uncertainty.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports venting the sealed section so a sun-heated box does not pull water in during rain.
