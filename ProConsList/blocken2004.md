# blocken2004: A review of wind-driven rain research in building science

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jweia.2004.06.003
**Evidence boundary:** Review. Read in full as the author preprint.

## Sensors

Rain gauges on facades (reviewed studies).

### Pros

- Summarises measured and simulated wetting patterns.

### Cons

- Building scale.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Building facades with and without overhangs.

### Pros

- Windward top corners get the most rain, then top and side edges.
- The gauge under a roof overhang caught the least; the edge of the sheltered zone was hardest to predict.

### Cons

- No vent-specific numbers; drop paths around a small body differ.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports keeping openings away from the top windward edges and under an overhang.
