# ouimette2024: Fundamentals of low-cost aerosol sensor design and operation

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/02786826.2023.2285935
**Evidence boundary:** Lab measurements plus CFD. Read in full as the open author manuscript (PMC11236278).

## Sensors

Plantower PMS5003.

### Pros

- Measures the fan pressure and the effect of inlet restriction on the count.

### Cons

- Orifice tests were sealed to the sensor inlet.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

PurpleAir housing: PVC tube capped on top, open at the bottom, sensor inlet about 0.7 cm above the opening (CFD).

### Pros

- Fan pressure 3.5 Pa running, 3.9 Pa at zero flow.
- 1.0 Pa restriction cut the count by 30%; 2.5 Pa by 83%.
- Below 3 m/s wind, all modelled sizes up to 10 micrometres reach the sensor.

### Cons

- CFD is not checked against measured aspiration.
- Dust deposition seen in a two-year unit was not quantified.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports measuring PM count inside each vent configuration against open air.
- Cannot give a minimum hole size for the lab box.
