# freeth2015bsria: Weather Louvre Test L.066IM1

**Source:** https://dam.renson.net/m/3bfd7ce194657c91/original/MR074-L-066IM1-Airflow-Rain-BSRIA-E-25-03-15.pdf
**Evidence boundary:** Independent test-lab report commissioned by the manufacturer. Read in full (11 pages). The rain rate is stated inconsistently in the source (75 l/h in Sec. 2.1, 75 mm/h in the results).

## Sensors

None.

### Pros

- Measures rain rejection at several face velocities on one specimen.

### Cons

- One product.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

1015 x 1000 mm aluminium louver, 15 blades, 66 mm pitch, 45 degree blades, insect guard, drip tray; 13 m/s wind.

### Pros

- Rejection 98.2% at 0 m/s, 95.6% at 1.0, 79.0% at 1.5, 59.2% at 2.0 and 17.7% at 3.5 m/s face velocity (Sec. 3.1).

### Cons

- Fan-drawn air; a passive box has no suction, though wind pressure on an opening is not zero.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports the mechanism that pressure difference across an opening drives water through it.
