# tse2025: Evaluating PurpleAir Sensors: Do They Accurately Reflect Ambient Air Temperature?

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/s25103044
**Evidence boundary:** Field comparison. Read in full. The WiFi-module heat attribution is the authors' inference.

## Sensors

BME280 in PurpleAir PA-II-SD; HOBO MX2301A references in shields.

### Pros

- 261,310 paired observations at 47 sites.

### Cons

- Reference and PurpleAir sites not identical.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

PurpleAir housing with BME280 under the cap, above the PM sensors.

### Pros

- Mean warm bias 3.77 C, MAE 4.71 C, RMSE 6.30 C.
- Bias largest at night and early morning; slightly negative mid-afternoon.

### Cons

- One product, one climate.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports keeping the T/RH sensor away from board heat and testing with electronics on and off.
