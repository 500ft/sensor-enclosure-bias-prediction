# staliulionis2016: Moisture ingress into electronics enclosures under isothermal conditions

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4951797
**Evidence boundary:** Modelling paper. Read in full.

## Sensors

None.

### Pros

- Gives a diffusion time constant for moisture through an opening.

### Cons

- Diffusion only, constant temperature.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Generic enclosure with one opening.

### Pros

- Time constant = volume x opening length / (opening area x vapour diffusivity).
- Fast outdoor swings average out inside; slow ones are followed.

### Cons

- Ignores convection, so it gives the still-air lag only.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports the drain and breather design for the sealed electronics section.
