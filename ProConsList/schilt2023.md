# schilt2023: Low-Cost Sensor Node for Air Quality Monitoring: Field Tests and Validation of Particulate Matter Measurements

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.3390/s23020794
**Evidence boundary:** Field validation. Read via Europe PMC. One open box against two closed boxes over different periods.

## Sensors

Low-cost PM sensor (model in paper).

### Pros

- Compares closed and open boxes against a reference.

### Cons

- No replication; date ranges differ.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Water-resistant plastic box with external snorkel and several base-plate holes (size and count not given).

### Pros

- Closed boxes overestimated PM10 (r 0.74 and 0.72); the open box in a ventilated cabinet had r 0.82 and slope closest to 1.

### Cons

- Confounded comparison.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Hints that a closed box affects PM readings; supports the in-box against open-air test.
