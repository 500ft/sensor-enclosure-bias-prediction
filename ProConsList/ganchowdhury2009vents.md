# ganchowdhury2009vents: Roof and Wall Vents Study under Simulated Hurricane Winds

**Source:** https://ihrc.fiu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/05/HLMP_Year09_Section2_Roof_WallVents_RCMP08-09.pdf
**Evidence boundary:** Full-scale test report. Read in full.

## Sensors

Water collection.

### Pros

- Varies wind angle 0 to 90 degrees for several vent types.

### Cons

- One specimen per vent, 3 minute exposure.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Gooseneck, turbine, ridge, soffit and gable-louver vents on gable and hip roofs; 19 in/h rain.

### Pros

- Water entry depends on pressure difference and the vent mechanism.
- The gooseneck took the most water when its opening faced the wind.
- Soffit vents took no water at any angle.

### Cons

- The soffit result contradicts Masters 2006; the authors call it unusual.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports facing openings away from the prevailing wind and testing several angles.
