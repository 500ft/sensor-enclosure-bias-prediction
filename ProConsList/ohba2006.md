# ohba2006: Experimental study on predicting wind-driven cross-ventilation flow rates and discharge coefficients based on the local dynamic similarity model

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/14733315.2006.11683728
**Evidence boundary:** Wind-tunnel experiment. Read in full from the authors' lab copy.

## Sensors

Thermal flow meter for ventilation rate.

### Pros

- Measures inflow and outflow coefficients across wind angles and opening positions.

### Cons

- Scaled building model.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

About 200 mm cube with 60 x 40 mm or 40 x 20 mm openings; boundary-layer flow at 7.0 m/s; wind angles 0 to 67.5 degrees.

### Pros

- Inflow coefficient follows one curve in dimensionless room pressure; it drops sharply when wind sweeps along the face.
- At 67.5 degrees, inflow 0.45 and outflow 0.55 against the usual fixed 0.67.
- Low windward openings sat below the common curve.

### Cons

- Single large openings, no hole arrays or screens.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports placing openings where wind does not mostly sweep along the face, and testing several wind angles.
