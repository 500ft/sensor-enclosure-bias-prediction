### Cons

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Generic enclosure.

### Pros

- Mount the inlet/outlet face flush with the wall or fit a shield between inlet and outlet.
- Enclosure opening no smaller than the sensor inlet.

### Cons

- No test data.
