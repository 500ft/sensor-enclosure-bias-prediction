# burt2022: Measurements of natural airflow within a Stevenson screen and its influence on air temperature and humidity records

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.5194/gi-11-263-2022
**Evidence boundary:** Field measurement. Read in full (CC BY).

## Sensors

Sonic anemometer inside the screen; thermometers.

### Pros

- 101 days of airflow measured inside a naturally ventilated housing.

### Cons

- One site and one screen design.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Louvred Stevenson screen 50 x 25 x 43 cm at Reading, UK.

### Pros

- Mean in-screen airflow 0.2 m/s, about 7% of the 10 m wind.
- In-screen air was calm (below 0.025 m/s) 10.6% of the time.
- Sensor response time scales with airflow to the -0.5 power.

### Cons

- Double louvres, about twice the lab box size.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports measuring the inside-to-outside wind ratio for each vent configuration.
- Cannot give that ratio for a box with holes.

Used in the [vent configuration review](../literature/vent_configuration_review.md).
