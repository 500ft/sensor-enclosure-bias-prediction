# karava2004: Wind driven flow through openings: a review of discharge coefficients

**Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/14733315.2004.11683920
**Evidence boundary:** Review. Journal version seen as abstract only; numbers are from the authors' full conference version (venue unverified).

## Sensors

None.

### Pros

- Collects wind-tunnel, full-scale and CFD discharge coefficients.

### Cons

- Building-scale openings.

## Physical Box, Materials, and Geometry

Building openings of many shapes; no small enclosures.

### Pros

- Orifice equation valid only for openings above about 10 mm.
- Discharge coefficient fell from 0.58 to 0.1 as wind angle went from 0 to 90 degrees (Fig. 4).
- Studies differ by up to 50% at open areas below 15%.

### Cons

- Millimetre vent holes fall outside the range it reviews.
- Several openings in one face had not been studied.

## Selection Lessons for This Project

- Supports treating hole count and size as untested at box scale.
- Supports wind angle as a test variable.
